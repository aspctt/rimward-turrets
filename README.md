## Description

Adds new turrets to your defenses. Formerly 40mm Bofors.

### 40mm Bofors Turret

A fully automatic take on the uranium slug turret. It fires the same uranium slugs, but in fast two-shot bursts.

* **Research** - bofors turret, after the uranium slug turret. Needs a hi-tech research bench and a multi-analyzer.
* **Building** - 2x2, uses 250 W, can break down and may explode when damaged.
* **Cost** - 500 steel, 40 plasteel, 40 uranium, 6 components and 2 advanced components.
* **Ammo** - holds 40 shots and is rearmed with uranium, one per shot.
* **Range** - 45.9 tiles, more accurate the further out it shoots, and can't fire at anything closer than 5.9 tiles.

It's balanced around Combat Extended, so it can feel a bit strong in vanilla.

## Installation

Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3650133609), or place the rimward-turrets folder from the release zip inside your RimWorld `Mods` folder.

**REMOVE ANY OLD VERSIONS BEFORE INSTALLING**.

## Compatibility

No dependencies. Works with any of these when they're installed:

* [Combat Extended](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2890901044) - the bofors fires Combat Extended's 40x311mmR rounds from a 40-round magazine, reaches 82 tiles, uses 650 W and doesn't explode when damaged.
* Better Architect Menu - the turrets go in its weapons category.
